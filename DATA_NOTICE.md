# Data notice

This release redistributes only processed labelled feature matrices and class-separated CDS sequences for *Plasmodium falciparum* 3D7, *P. berghei* ANKA, *P. knowlesi* H and *Toxoplasma gondii* GT1. Underlying reference proteomes and annotations derive from VEuPathDB release 68. The label resources were harmonised from Zhang et al. (2018; *P. falciparum* saturation mutagenesis), Bushell et al. (2017; *P. berghei* PlasmoGEM functional profiling), the 2025 *P. knowlesi* essential-genome data release, and Sidik et al. (2016; *T. gondii* genome-wide CRISPR screen).

Use remains subject to the licences, attribution requirements and terms of the original providers. No manuscript, genome-scale prediction atlas, target-prioritisation output, docking output, figure source data or unpublished result table is redistributed here.

The public deployment artefacts are not the locked formal manuscript model. See `docs/FORMAL_MODEL_PROTOCOL.md`.
